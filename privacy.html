HEX Camera Access Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

HEX Camera Access is a personal home dashboard application used by the owner to access and display their own Google Nest camera devices.

Information accessed

The application may access Google Nest device information and camera streams only after authorization by the owner.

How information is used

Information is used only to provide camera status and viewing functions within the owner's personal HEX dashboard.

Data sharing

HEX Camera Access does not sell, rent, or share Google user data with third parties.

Data storage

Authentication credentials and tokens used by HEX Camera Access are stored locally on the owner's private HEX computer and are not published on this website.
